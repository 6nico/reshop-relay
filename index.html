Integrity checks

What this is (and isn't)

  • Fictional store. The layout, staff, and tasks are made up. No real retailer data is used.
  • Department level only. The model knows which department an item belongs to, not its exact shelf spot.
  • Forum-informed. Cart limits and collection timing are based on what store workers describe online. Volumes and handling times are assumptions to test. See sources.
  • A strategy only "wins" if it passes the guardrails: no more errors, no late tasks, no bigger backlog at close, and no older items left waiting.
