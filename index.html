The question

Can stores return reshop with less labor?

Reshop piles up at Guest Services all day. Today, a team member makes a separate trip to put it back. This study tests a simple idea: pick up reshop during trips team members already make, like breaks and backup cashier. The goal is to cut labor without hurting service.

How we tested it

  • Fair test. All three ways get the same reshop, staff, and tasks, so only the method changes.
  • Guardrails. A method only counts if it has no more errors, no late tasks, no bigger pile at close, and no older items.
  • Fictional store. Layout and staff are made up. Cart limits and timing come from what store workers report online. Sources

What counts as a win

  • At least 20% less labor per item returned
  • No more items left at close
  • No more placement errors
  • No late freight, breaks, or backup cashier

Before starting

  • Get approval from store leadership
  • Only use company data with permission
  • Tell the team what is being tracked and why
  • Track the process, not individual people
